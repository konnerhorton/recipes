# Pressure Cooker Times

| Ingredient   | Time       | Release | Pressure | Notes  |
| ------------ | ---------- | ------- | -------- | ------ |
| Kidney beans | 10 minutes |    natural     |      high    | soaked |
| Chickpeas | 15 minutes |    natural     |      high    | soaked |

> for soaking beans, use about equal parts salt and baking soda in the soaking water, rinse thoroughly before cooking.
